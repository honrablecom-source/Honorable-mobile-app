White mask RGB keeps source opacity, black removes it, and gray produces partial opacity. The encoded-channel calculation is `(0.2126R+0.7152G+0.0722B)/255`, optionally inverted before multiplying by mask alpha and source alpha. This is explicitly encoded RGB weighting, not a physically calibrated measurement or subject recognition. Transparent mask pixels remain transparent even when inverted. Source RGB stays unchanged wherever output alpha is nonzero; zero-alpha output has cleared RGB. Unchanged source opacity refuses a no-op duplicate.
