Titles & audio → Suggest audio-onset cuts scans the selected whole clip's original source audio locally, suggests energy-rise boundaries, lets the owner uncheck candidates, then reviews every resulting source window and selected-clip caption assignment before an owned one-use Apply and preview. One Undo restores the original edit. This differs from C079's removal of quiet pauses, C081's level inspection and the existing manual tempo-grid cuts: it uses decoded source energy to propose optional new edit boundaries. It is an experimental RMS-rise heuristic, not musical beat, speech, semantic or sample-exact recognition.

The existing VideoQuietAudio scanner accepts one readable audio track, at most two source minutes, bounded decoder packets/PCM formats, and a checked 30-second deadline. It releases every extractor/decoder/buffer. New controller owns setup/progress, one worker and atomic close, rejects late/account/session results and checks a 35-second total deadline. Provider/decoder blocking can delay physical cancellation. UI verifies exact current source maps/durations, selected clip, project identity/content, soundtrack, trim/close state, account namespace, foreground/recovery/busy state and generation. After releasing its own busy state, it admits results through the existing full caption/batch review boundary. Pause/destroy close analysis; existing batch stages own candidate/review callbacks and consume approval before mutation.

Detection uses exact contiguous 20 ms source-time RMS windows (short final window allowed), requiring at least 95% of duration to have known windows. The existing PCM scanner's known-window rules are not a claim of 95% exact sample completeness. The previous ten known windows provide a 200 ms history median, floored at 0.001 RMS. A candidate must be at least 0.02 RMS, twice that baseline, rise at least 0.01 above its predecessor, strictly exceed its predecessor and meet/exceed its successor. The candidate and both neighbors/history must be known. The first 200 source milliseconds provide history and are not candidates. Times are 20 ms window starts, not actual event onsets. Nearby alternatives keep the strongest peak/baseline ratio, then earliest time. Edges and potential cuts retain at least 160 playback milliseconds for the clip's speed. More than 200 accepted suggestions refuses the analysis rather than silently truncating it. Speech, music, noise and compression can cause false positives or misses. No confidence/beat-quality claim.

The owner chooses one or more suggestions. New source-contiguous pieces retain every original source millisecond, order, speed, gain, title/style, grade, layout, source motion anchors and subtitle references. Intersecting captions keep their full source times/text; cues wholly outside the old visible window disappear from this edit and are listed with no retained output positions. Other clips and soundtrack settings stay. Integer timing can contract by at most one microsecond per added piece; the complete effective timing is reviewed. Video/audio edge fades repeat per piece and can change appearance/audio; preview/listen before export. Caption/title/recovery/project limits refuse an oversized proposal before review. The full report (≤1 MiB) lists all chosen source timestamps/RMS/baselines, all resulting timeline source windows, and every selected-clip caption with retained output positions. Source IDs, not URI/filename/account metadata, are shown. Nothing changes during analysis or candidate selection.
