## Defined source contract

Source64–1024pixels wide and8–1024high; full decoded plane precedes canvas crop/rotation/flip/opacity. SpacingP16–128 with4P≤width. Seed is unsigned32-bit. Encoded luminance is `299R+587G+114B`; inversion replaces this with255000minus luminance before alpha. Integer depthD=luminance×alpha, normalized byQ=65025000. Transparency always means far in both polarities, and hidden transparent RGB cannot affect output. The displayed guide is round(255D/Q), excluded from PNG.

Virtual eye spacingE=2P, depth-of-field ratio1/3. Integer half-up pair separation is `round(2P(3Q-D)/(6Q-D))`. Alternate rows choose opposite rounding of odd separation halves. A candidate pair outside the image is clipped. Occlusion probes test the two rays until their depth exceeds the normalized near plane; exact integer comparisons avoid floating equality ambiguities. Visible pair constraints form one graph per row. Disjoint-set unions choose the maximum coordinate as canonical component representative. Every pixel in a component receives the same dot. Colors are the high bit of a32-bit LCG1664525/1013904223, advanced from right to left across every position; row initialization xors seed with `(row+1)*0x9e3779b1` modulo2^32. A seed changes colors only, not depth, visibility or connectivity. Counts record visible constraints, occluded/clipped pairs and components; none proves perceived depth.

## Bounded PNG

[PNG grayscale color-type0 permits one-bit samples](https://www.w3.org/TR/png-3/#11IHDR). The independent writer uses filter0, MSB-first bit rows, zero padding, IHDR/IDAT/IEND, CRC32, [zlib Adler32](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1950) and [stored DEFLATE blocks](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1951). This avoids an entropy-dependent CanvasPNG size without resizing or changing dots. Raw scanline bytes are `R=(ceil(width/8)+1)*height`; complete PNG size is `R+63+5*ceil(R/65535)`, at most132174bytes at1024square, below192KiB regardless of content. It is lossless but deliberately not compression-optimized. The helper still checks Blob type/size/exact expected length before publication. No parser, storage, MIME or file-count limit loosened.

PNG encoding privately captures bits, yields in16-row chunks and checks current ownership before/after final yield. The stereogram kernel privately captures RGBA and yields depth/constraint row chunks. The wrapper has a shared30second deadline across decode, constraints and PNG construction; Stop/hidden/abort/stale scene/unmount discard results. Insertion stores source ID, dimensions, spacing, seed, polarity, model convention and counts. Both source and output remain separate. Device/native decoder behavior and memory/performance are unverified; private RGBA/depth/root/bit/guide/PNG/decode/canvas buffers consume additional memory.
