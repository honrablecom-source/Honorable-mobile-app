## Implemented

- Photo editor → Adjust → Tone curve: monotone RGB curve with three editable interior points, graph, reset and explicit Apply/Cancel. Preview/export use the same renderer. Undo/redo, Compare, Reset and scoped pending-export/lifecycle state retain the parameters.
- Photo editor → Adjust → Local light: one feathered circular exposure adjustment with normalized source position, radius, feather and exposure (-2 to +2 EV). Position via sliders or Place on photo / tap. Source-coordinate mapping handles crop/quarter rotation; pixel alpha is retained, including person cutout. This is a local exposure mask, not a generative relighting/normal-map model.
- Photo editor → Assist → AI scene enhance / AI photo looks: existing bundled on-device scene labeling now combines with sampled luminance statistics, bounded exposure, RGB curves and color controls. Auto, Cinematic, Soft portrait, Vivid and Monochrome looks are available. Geometry/cutout/local-light settings remain; results reject stale edits. No source pixels, prompts or labels are uploaded. These are classifier-assisted looks, not generated photos.
- Video editor → Assist → AI rough cut: choose 15/30/60 seconds and Balanced/People/Scenery. For up to eight existing timeline clips, local labels rank four sampled frames per clip. Duration is distributed in playback time, retaining clip order, speed, volume, title/fades and soundtrack. The result is reviewed before one undoable draft application; original media remains protected by the existing exporter. Target duration may differ by a few milliseconds due to supported playback-rate rounding. No frames are synthesized when footage is shorter than the target.
- Cancel analysis: cancellation discards the result. Late results cannot replace changed clips/trim/source selection or soundtrack. At most 32 frame-label calls, five-second model waits and a 60-second checked budget bound the attempt; blocking native decoder/provider calls are not forcibly interruptible. No claims of speech understanding, beat synchronization or storytelling quality.
